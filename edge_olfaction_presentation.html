03 — ANALYTICAL ENGINE

Predictive Volatile Indexing

IEEE-aligned mathematical normalization transforming raw chemiresistor resistance into deterministic decay metrics.

1. BASELINE RESISTANCE SHIFT (DRIFT CANCELLATION)
$$S_i(t) = \frac{V_i(t) - V_{i,\text{baseline}}}{V_{i,\text{baseline}}}$$

Compensates for sensor aging and ambient temperature variations using baseline Kalman tracking.

2. MULTI-CHANNEL VOLATILE EMISSION INDEX (VEI)
$$\text{VEI}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{4} w_i \cdot \tanh\left(\alpha_i \cdot S_i(t)\right)$$

Bounded non-linear saturation preventing outliers from skewing composite spoilage vectors.
